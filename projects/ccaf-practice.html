Results you can act on

01

Every exam ends with a scaled score from 100 to 1000 against the 720 pass mark, the accuracy for each of the five domains, and an explanation for every question, including why the other options are wrong.

  • Pick 3, 5, 10, 20 or 40 questions drawn in the blueprint’s proportions, run the 60-question mock in scenario order, or build a custom exam from the sources you choose.
  • Missed questions are collected automatically and clear themselves when you get them right in a later exam.
  • A readiness check looks at your last three exams and every domain before it calls you ready.
